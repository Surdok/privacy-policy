# Jira Case Assistant privacy policy

Jira Case Assistant is a Chrome extension for the Sijilat support team's internal use.

## What the extension reads

- On the team's Jira site, the key of the case being viewed (for example `SS-1234`).
- Text the user selects and chooses to ask about from the right-click menu, and questions typed into the side panel.
- The user's settings for the extension.

## Where that data goes

- Case keys and questions are sent only to the team's own internal Utility API Server, which looks the case up in Jira
  and answers from the team's internal knowledge base. They are not sent to the extension's developer or to anyone
  else.
- The server keeps answers for up to 12 hours so that other team members viewing the same case get them faster.
- Settings are saved with Chrome's storage, which Chrome may sync to the user's other devices when they are signed in
  to Chrome.

## What the extension does not do

- It does not collect personal information, browsing history, or analytics.
- It does not run on any site other than the team's Jira and the team's API server.
- It does not sell or share data, and does not use it for any purpose other than the one above.
